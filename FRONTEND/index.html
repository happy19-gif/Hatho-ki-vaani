Questions, answered

Does my video leave my device?

No video is uploaded by this app. The hand-tracking pipeline runs in your browser. Speaking a caption sends the caption text, not video, to your configured speech service.

Why is my sign not recognised?

Demo mode only produces sample predictions. For real recognition, install a trained model that matches the selected sign language and landmark input shape. Lighting and camera framing can also affect tracking.

Which sign language should I pick?

Choose the language used by the people communicating with you. ISL and ASL are distinct languages, not interchangeable gestures.

How do I change the voice?

Use Female voice or Male voice in the caption panel. The browser fallback varies by device; configured ElevenLabs voices require voice IDs and a server environment key.
